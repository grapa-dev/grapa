Research components explore advanced concepts, experimental features, and innovative approaches to programming language design.

- **Innovation**: Exploration of cutting-edge programming language concepts

## Development Guidelines

When working with research components:
1. **Concept Development**: Document innovative concepts with clear rationale
2. **Experimental Features**: Track experimental implementations and results
3. **Future Planning**: Maintain roadmap for research-to-implementation transition
4. **Innovation Tracking**: Document successful and unsuccessful experiments
