# Programming Language Naming Investigation (Final — Decision to Retain "Grapa")

**Date**: August 2025  
**Status**: Decision Finalized — Stay with Grapa  
**Purpose**: Confirm decision to retain the "Grapa" brand and outline the action plan to strengthen brand presence, improve discoverability, and lock in namespace ownership.

---

## 1. Decision Summary

After evaluating Grapa against potential alternatives (Gramaris, Lexora) using availability checks, search perception analysis, SEO impact, and community transition costs, **Grapa** remains the optimal choice.

**Reasons to Stay with Grapa**:
- **Zero migration cost** — no risk of losing existing SEO or confusing current users.
- **Clear namespace control** — GitHub `grapa-dev` already secured; likely available on PyPI.
- **Low collision risk** — Minimal unrelated search results for "Grapa programming language."
- **Thematic relevance** — Derived from "grammar + parser," directly tied to core technical differentiator.

---

## 2. Web Search & Wikipedia Considerations

## 4. Action Plan — Strengthening "Grapa"

### **Step 1 — Lock in Namespace Ownership**
- Register `grapa.com`, `grapa.dev`, and `grapa.io` (redirect to main site/docs).
- Reserve `grapa` on PyPI to protect the name.
- Maintain `grapa-dev` GitHub org and optionally claim `grapa` if available.

### **Step 2 — Clarify Brand Story**
- Add a **"Name Origin & Pronunciation"** section to the About page:
  > **Grapa** (pronounced "GRAP-uh") comes from **GRAmmer + PArser** — highlighting the language’s grammar-first design.
- Use a tagline in site headers, repo descriptions, and social profiles:
  > *Grapa — grammar-first scripting for data & ETL.*

### **Step 3 — Strengthen SEO & Discoverability**
- Optimize for "Grapa programming language" and "grammar-based scripting" keywords.
- Publish 2–3 technical blog posts or tutorials that link back to the official site and docs.
- Cross-post to developer forums and platforms (Reddit r/programming, Dev.to, Hacker News).

### **Step 4 — Prepare for Wikipedia**
- Build independent coverage:
  - Secure at least 2–3 articles or mentions in credible publications or developer blogs.
  - Collect citations from conference talks, academic references, or major open-source usage.
- Draft Wikipedia article once notability threshold is met.

### **Step 5 — Maintain Consistent Branding**
- Ensure all official assets (GitHub repos, PyPI, docs, social profiles) use **Grapa** consistently.
- Create a brand kit (logo, color palette, tagline usage) for internal and public use.
